## Case inspection

The [Yonhap report](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261030090400001), [SBS report](https://news.sbs.co.kr/amp/news.amp?news_id=N1008823127) and [Electronic Times report](https://www.etnews.com/20261030000210) describe the same October 30 meeting between the ruling party and virtual-asset industry about tax deferral. The SBS/Yonhap pair was confirmed; Electronic Times remained provisional because the title similarity did not pass the conservative threshold. This is a real missed connection, not proof of statistically significant recall. Similar reporting does not establish that the reporting or its underlying evidence is independent. The Yonhap page states that unauthorized AI use is prohibited; no original text from this case was sent to Gemini by the new path.

The report and pair labels are not an independently human-reviewed test set. The selected case cannot establish precision, recall, F1 or Recall@K. Those metrics remain unavailable for the 446-candidate dataset until independently reviewed labels are supplied. The preliminary review queue is [here](shadow-clustering-2026-10-30.md).
